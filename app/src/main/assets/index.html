Kuro OS, a Y2K home screen

Swipe the phone sideways for page 2. Long-press the empty wallpaper for the right-click menu, and use Start to change the wallpaper or upload sprites. Everything you upload stays in this browser.

Your wallpaper stays yours

A launcher draws its widgets on top of whatever wallpaper you set, the same way your current home screen does. In the real app you keep your wallpaper and only the widgets and icons change. You can test that here with Start → Wallpaper.

Works on your Samsung

  • Your own launcher as the home screen (set it as default; no root)
  • The head unit controlling Spotify (play, skip, track name, through Android's media controls)
  • Aizawa and Mic with 3 sprites each, talking, petting, OK and snooze buttons
  • Lines that react to the time, your battery and your to-do list
  • Pixel icons for any app, Start menu, notepad, cat game, click sounds and vibration

Needs a workaround

  • A spectrum that follows the real music (needs microphone permission; otherwise it animates while music plays)
  • Album covers and liked songs (needs a free Spotify developer key)
  • Samsung closing the app in the background (set battery use to "Unrestricted")

Not possible

  • Restyling the status bar and pull-down panel
  • Characters seeing what happens inside other apps
